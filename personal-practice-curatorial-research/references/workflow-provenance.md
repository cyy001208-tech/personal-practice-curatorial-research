## Direct methodological influence

### Deep Research Skill

**Source:** Supervisor-Skills contributors,
[`skills/deep-research/SKILL.md`](https://github.com/HKUSTDial/Supervisor-Skills/blob/aff5de9e5b902df0ef51e955d4c78b22793d763a/skills/deep-research/SKILL.md),
licensed `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0` in the cited skill frontmatter.

This repository adapts the following workflow principles:

- freeze the research brief and define answerable questions before broad
  collection;
- search or inspect from multiple perspectives rather than building one
  preferred answer immediately;
- verify references before using them as evidence;
- keep an inspectable corpus and intermediate research artifacts;
- use explicit quality gates that can send the work back to targeted evidence
  collection;
- calibrate the strength of a conclusion to the strength and range of its
  evidence.

The local implementation changes the research object. `Deep Research` is a
survey-grade literature workflow; `personal-practice-curatorial-research`
studies a completed project archive, project events, situated testimony,
collaboration, reception, and absence as a curatorial case.

## Consulted boundary

### Idea Evaluator Skill

**Source:** Supervisor-Skills contributors,
[`skills/idea-evaluator/SKILL.md`](https://github.com/HKUSTDial/Supervisor-Skills/blob/aff5de9e5b902df0ef51e955d4c78b22793d763a/skills/idea-evaluator/SKILL.md),
licensed `CC-BY-4.0` in the cited skill frontmatter.

`Idea Evaluator` was consulted to define a boundary, not imported as the
assessment model. This repository does **not** reuse its future-facing idea
scores, lifecycle verdicts, or Strong Accept / Accept with Revisions / Reject
and Pivot outcomes. A completed practice is evaluated retrospectively through
documented work, role, method, evidence, counterevidence, and uncertainty. The
skill does not score the attractiveness of a future idea or the value of a
person.
